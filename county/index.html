Governments/County

Tom Green County

The County runs the courts, the jail, the Sheriff's Office, county roads, elections, and property records. The Commissioners Court, four commissioners and the County Judge, sets the budget and the tax rate.

CityCountySAISDAppraisal DistrictDevelopment Corporation
The bottom line

The County is spending down its savings, from $39.4 million to $21.9 million this year. Past meeting agendas and several required reports are not online.27

Key numbers

Debt $43.95M

Owed on Aug 31, 2026, after a 2025 refinancing.10

Budget gap -$17.4M

FY2027 spending over revenue, mostly one-time projects.2

Reserves $39.4M to $21.9M

The cushion the budget spends down this year.2

Tax rate $0.46825

Per $100 for FY2027, down slightly, but above the break-even rate.211

What to know

7 of 3,256

Almost no dissent

Recorded votes since January 2020 with a no vote. Nearly everything passes unanimously or on consent.1

+34%

Commissioners' pay

The salary line for commissioners for FY2027, after a 3% raise for all elected officials, a $14,000 base raise for commissioners, and part of their car allowance moved into salary.23

Since FY2007

Outside auditor

The same firm has audited the County since at least FY2007. Since FY2016 the contract has been renewed each year without competitive bids. The FY2025 audit found no problems.34

Internal audits

Open questions (5)

What the record does not answer yet, and who can answer it.Open
Debt Not yet asked

How much of the debt still owed was approved by voters?

What the record shows
Since 2020 the City has issued certificates of obligation and tax notes, which need no election unless 5% of voters petition. The state's debt data does not say which debt was voted on.89
Who can answer
City Finance Department, County Auditor
Audits Not yet asked

When will the County next seek competitive bids for its outside auditor?

What the record shows
The same firm has audited the County since at least FY2007. Since FY2016 the contract has been renewed yearly under a professional-services exemption, without bids.3
Who can answer
Commissioners Court, County Judge
Audits Not yet asked

Why have internal audit reviews dropped, and when will FY2026 reviews be posted?

What the record shows
The County Auditor's page lists 38 review letters in FY2021 and FY2022, 3 in FY2025, and none for FY2026.5
Who can answer
County Auditor
Records Not yet asked

Will the County keep past Commissioners Court agendas online?

What the record shows
Only the current week's agenda is posted, and the file is replaced each week. Several standing reports stopped updating, some in 2021.7
Who can answer
County Clerk, County Judge
Contracts Not yet asked

Why was the $450,000 settlement amount left out of the minutes?

What the record shows
The Court approved a negotiated settlement on June 3, 2025. The minutes give no amount. The check register shows a $450,000 settlement payment on Aug 27, 2025.36
Who can answer
County Attorney, County Judge
