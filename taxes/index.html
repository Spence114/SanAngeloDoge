Why a lower rate can cost you more

The one idea that explains most tax-rate debates.

  1. Values go up. The Appraisal District raises property values.
  2. The break-even rate. The state makes each government calculate a "no-new-revenue" rate: the rate that would bring in the same money as last year from the same properties.
  3. Above it means more money. Any rate above the break-even rate raises more from the same homes and businesses, even if it is lower than last year's rate.
