Investments City Not yet asked

Where are the City's quarterly investment reports?

What the record shows
The City's investment policy, re-adopted each year, says investment reports are presented to Council at least quarterly and reviewed by an oversight committee. We found none on any Council agenda from 2017 to 2026.12
Who can answer
City Finance Department, City Manager
Audits City Not yet asked

When did the Investment Oversight Committee and the Audit Committee last meet?

What the record shows
Members were appointed to both. No agendas or minutes for either are online, and the Audit Committee's portal page shows no meetings.2
Who can answer
City Clerk
Debt CityCounty Not yet asked

How much of the debt still owed was approved by voters?

What the record shows
Since 2020 the City has issued certificates of obligation and tax notes, which need no election unless 5% of voters petition. The state's debt data does not say which debt was voted on.23
Who can answer
City Finance Department, County Auditor
Debt City Not yet asked

What did the bonds behind the City's $53.8 million in refunding debt originally pay for?

What the record shows
The state lists $53.8 million of City debt as refunding: new bonds sold to pay off older ones. It does not say what the older bonds built.4
Who can answer
City Finance Department
Debt City Not yet asked

Which streets and projects does the City's $67.0 million in transportation debt cover?

What the record shows
The state's category is transportation. The City's certificates of obligation in 2020 and 2022 were for streets. A project list is not in the state data.24
Who can answer
City Finance Department, Public Works
Debt SAISD Not yet asked

Why does SAISD list $473.3 million in obligations when its bonds total $446.8 million?

What the record shows
The district's transparency page gives $473.3 million in total obligations as of Aug 31, 2025. Its debt report lists $446.8 million in bonds.56
Who can answer
SAISD Financial Services
Investments SAISD Not yet asked

Where does the interest earned on unspent bond money go?

What the record shows
$361.5 million of bond money sat in the construction fund on Sep 30, 2026, earning about $1.2 million that month. Federal rules can require earnings above the bond rate to be paid back.7
Who can answer
SAISD Financial Services
Schools SAISD Not yet asked

What enrollment forecast were the new school buildings sized for?

What the record shows
Enrollment fell from 14,100 in 2021 to 13,004 in 2025, as the district reports it, while voters approved $397 million for new and rebuilt schools in May 2025.58
Who can answer
SAISD Superintendent, Board of Trustees
Debt SAISD Not yet asked

How much of the $397 million voters approved can still be borrowed?

What the record shows
SAISD sold $385.1 million of the $397 million authorized, plus a premium of about $16.9 million. Whether the premium counts against the authorization decides what is left.68
Who can answer
SAISD Financial Services
Audits County Not yet asked

When will the County next seek competitive bids for its outside auditor?

What the record shows
The same firm has audited the County since at least FY2007. Since FY2016 the contract has been renewed yearly under a professional-services exemption, without bids.9
Who can answer
Commissioners Court, County Judge
Audits County Not yet asked

Why have internal audit reviews dropped, and when will FY2026 reviews be posted?

What the record shows
The County Auditor's page lists 38 review letters in FY2021 and FY2022, 3 in FY2025, and none for FY2026.10
Who can answer
County Auditor
Records County Not yet asked

Will the County keep past Commissioners Court agendas online?

What the record shows
Only the current week's agenda is posted, and the file is replaced each week. Several standing reports stopped updating, some in 2021.11
Who can answer
County Clerk, County Judge
Contracts County Not yet asked

Why was the $450,000 settlement amount left out of the minutes?

What the record shows
The Court approved a negotiated settlement on June 3, 2025. The minutes give no amount. The check register shows a $450,000 settlement payment on Aug 27, 2025.912
Who can answer
County Attorney, County Judge
Privacy City Not yet asked

Is statewide or national sharing turned on for San Angelo's Flock cameras, and how long are records kept?

What the record shows
Flock lets each agency choose whether other agencies can search its plate records, and how many days records are kept. The City's packet does not say which settings SAPD uses.1314
Who can answer
San Angelo Police Department
Records Appraisal District Not yet asked

Will the Appraisal District post its adopted budget, audits, and board minutes?

What the record shows
Its proposed budget and annual reports are posted. Its adopted budget, audits, and board minutes were not, and the website was down on Oct 28, 2026.1516
Who can answer
Chief Appraiser, Board of Directors
Incentives Development Corporation Not yet asked

What did each Development Corporation award promise, and was it delivered?

What the record shows
Monthly statements list the payments, such as $1 million to Angelo State University and $500,000 to Skyline Aviation. They do not list what each recipient agreed to do or whether it did.17
Who can answer
Development Corporation board and staff
