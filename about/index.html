About San Angelo DOGE

An independent, citizen-run look at local government finances in San Angelo and Tom Green County: the City of San Angelo, Tom Green County, San Angelo ISD, and the Tom Green County Appraisal District.

Who runs it

San Angelo DOGE is run by Spencer Matthews, a San Angelo resident and independent journalist. It has no political party and is not affiliated with any government, or with any organization using the name DOGE at the federal or state level. The author has no financial interest in the data center projects covered here. His work building AI systems is disclosed below.

The goal is simple: local government works better when residents can see the numbers. Budgets, debt, votes, and big projects are presented in plain words, with every figure linked to where it came from.

Disclosure

Spencer Matthews, who writes this site, builds AI systems for businesses and organizations through his company, Mosir.

He is also helping the San Angelo Police Department decide how to choose a local company to build the City-owned plate camera system described on our Flock cameras page.

How we work

Sources
Figures come from public records: adopted budgets, audits, state debt reports, agendas and minutes, permit filings, and company filings. Each figure links to its source or names it.
Pink
Pink marks anything not yet verified against a source document: estimates, projections, company statements, and items from news coverage.
Same rules for all
The four governments get the same scrutiny and the same format.
Privacy
We hold that privacy is a human right. It shapes how we handle your information and how we cover surveillance. Our position on plate cameras and our proposed Technology Bill of Rights are on the Flock cameras page.
Data and opinion apart
Data pages stick to the record. Analysis and opinion are labeled as such and kept apart.
Conflicts shown
When two official documents disagree, we show both and list the question for the office that published them.
Corrections
Errors are fixed and logged below with the date.

Your privacy on this site

What we collect. Only what you type into a form: your email, and if you choose, your name, phone, street address, topics, and messages. Tip line contact fields are optional.

Why. To send the alerts you asked for, to measure zoning cases against your address, and to answer you.

Who sees it. Forms are sent by email to Spencer@sanangelodoge.com. Only the San Angelo DOGE team reads them. We never sell or share your information, and we never publish a tipster's name without permission.

Your choices. Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your information deleted, email Spencer@sanangelodoge.com.
