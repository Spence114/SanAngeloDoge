Tip line

Report waste, wrongdoing, or an error

If you work for a local government, do business with one, or saw something, tell us. Spotted a wrong number on this site? Send that here too. We check everything against the public record before anything is published, and we never publish your name without your permission.

What are you sending?
If we contact you

Before you send

  • Use a personal phone or computer on a home network, not a work device, work email, or work Wi-Fi.
  • Do not send your only copy of anything.
  • Files can carry your name in hidden details. Describing a document, or where to find it, is often safer than sending it.

What we do with it

We read every tip. We confirm it through public records and records requests before publishing anything. If we cannot confirm it, it is not published. Corrections are checked against the source document and logged on the About page.

Legal protection

Texas law protects public employees who report a violation of law in good faith to an appropriate law enforcement authority (Texas Government Code, Chapter 554). A tip to a journalist is not that kind of report, so this tip line cannot give you that protection. If you want it, also report to the right authority, and talk to a lawyer first.
