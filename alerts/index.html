How it works

Before, notice meant a postcard and a legal ad in the newspaper. Now, when the City posts a case on its website, we post it and email you.

  1. 1

    The City posts it

    Zoning cases and ordinances go up on the City's agendas and website before the hearings.

  2. 2

    Our agent reads it

    Software picks up each new case, reads the documents, scanned ones included, and records the address, the change asked for, and the hearing dates.

  3. 3

    We map it

    Each case goes on the map and is measured against every subscriber's address.

  4. 4

    You get an email

    What would change, where, the hearing dates, and how to speak. The case is posted on this site too.

Why the legal notice is not enough

What Texas law requires a city to do before a zoning vote.

Letters
Mailed only to owners of property within 200 feet, as listed on the tax roll, at least 10 days before the zoning commission hearing. Renters do not get one.1
Published notice
In a newspaper and on the City's website, before the 15th day before the City Council hearing.2
Signs
Since September 2025, a sign at least 24 by 48 inches on the property, or in the right-of-way when the City proposes a change covering many properties, at least 10 days before the zoning commission hearing.2
Large changes
For a comprehensive change, such as a new zoning code, a new zoning map, or an overlay district, the published notices are the only ones the law requires. No letters.2

Large rezonings have caught San Angelo neighborhoods off guard before, and residents have had to organize on short notice to be heard. Everyone near a change should know about it in time to speak.
