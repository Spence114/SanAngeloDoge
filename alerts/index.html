Zoning alerts

Know before they vote

The City only has to mail notice of a zoning change to owners within 200 feet.1 Renters, neighbors one block over, and businesses down the street hear about it after the vote. We send you every case near the places you care about, in plain words, with the hearing dates.

Get early accessHow it works

Sign-up open Alerts are in development. Early sign-ups are first in line.

Built for everyone near a change

A zoning change can raise traffic, noise, and property values, or lower them. Everyone nearby has a stake.

Homeowners

Hear about rezonings, new apartments, and commercial projects near your street, not just next door.

Renters

The law sends letters to owners only. Get the same notice your landlord gets.

Rental owners

Watch every property you own from one account, so nothing changes around a rental without you knowing.

Businesses

See what is coming to your block: competitors, road changes, and new rules that affect your location.

  • On the map

    Each case is mapped and measured against your addresses.

  • Hearing dates and how to speak

    When the zoning commission and Council will hear it, and how to comment or show up.

  • Email or text

    Get alerts by email, or by text for the ones closest to you.

  • Many addresses, one account

    For rental owners, businesses, and anyone watching more than one place.

How it works

Before, notice meant a postcard and a legal ad. Now, when the City posts a case, we post it and tell you.

  1. 1

    The City posts it

    Zoning cases and ordinances go up on the City's agendas and website before the hearings.

  2. 2

    Our agent reads it

    Software picks up each new case, reads the documents, scanned ones included, and records the address, the change asked for, and the hearing dates.

  3. 3

    We map it

    Each case goes on the map and is measured against every subscriber's address.

  4. 4

    You get an email

    What would change, where, the hearing dates, and how to speak. The case is posted on this site too.

Why the legal notice is not enough

What Texas law requires before a zoning vote.

Letters
Mailed only to owners of property within 200 feet, as listed on the tax roll, at least 10 days before the zoning commission hearing. Renters do not get one.1
Published notice
In a newspaper and on the City's website, before the 15th day before the City Council hearing.2
Signs
Since September 2025, a sign on the property, or in the right-of-way when the City proposes a change covering many properties, at least 10 days before the zoning commission hearing.2
Large changes
For a new zoning code, a new zoning map, or an overlay district, the published notices are the only ones the law requires. No letters.2

Questions

Is this from the City?

No. San Angelo DOGE is independent. We use the cases the City already posts publicly, and send them to you faster and in plainer words.

When do alerts start?

They are in development now. Sign up and you will be on the list when the first alerts go out.

Does this cover the county?

We start with City of San Angelo zoning cases, where the City has zoning power. Tom Green County does not zone land outside city limits.

What do you do with my address?

We use it only to measure cases against it. We never sell or share it.
