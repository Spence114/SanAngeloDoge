Large costs and commitments beyond bonded debt. Pink text rests on news coverage until we check the source document.

    Owed beyond what the plans hold, Sep 30, 2025. The Firemen's Relief and Retirement Fund was 60.7% funded at its Dec 31, 2023 valuation and would take 34 years to close its gap. The state Pension Review Board has listed it as at risk of needing a funding restoration plan since at least July 2025, and it was still on that list in July 2026.222627

    Bond money not yet spent

    $361,509,892

    Left in the construction fund on Sep 30, 2026, from the $397 million bond voters approved in May 2025. The $385.1 million borrowed so far adds $424.5 million in interest and is repaid through 2055.62829

    $171,939,200

    Capital plan estimates approved Jul 6, 2023: $62.4 million for the water treatment plant, $55.5 million for water reclamation, and $54.0 million for the Lake Nasworthy sewer. Part of the sewer work was financed with bonds in 2023, so some of this cost may already be in the debt figures above.30313233

How to read these numbers

Owed is what was borrowed and not yet repaid. Interest still to pay is the interest on the repayment schedule from the report date to the last payment. Together they are the total future payments.

Local debt does not grow every second the way the federal debt does. Principal goes down as payments are made and jumps when new bonds are sold. The interest counter spreads this year's scheduled interest evenly across the year so you can see the pace.

Some official documents disagree with each other. The proposed dashboard would show both figures and the question for the office that published them.
