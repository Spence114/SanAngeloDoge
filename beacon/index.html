Beacon data center

A data center with its own natural gas power plant, proposed about a quarter mile south of US 67 on FM 2335, along Spring Creek near Dove Creek, about 10 miles southwest of San Angelo. The plant would power only the data center. It sits outside city limits, where the county cannot zone or regulate noise.

At a glance

Plant figures come from the applicant's own filings with TCEQ. Gas use is our estimate, because the filings do not state it.

Developer
Beacon Data Centers. The permit applicant is Westline TX Holdings LLC, and the state filings call the project Westline 2335.34
Ownership
Started by Nadia Partners, which trade press describes as a Calgary-based venture firm. Investors not disclosed.567
Air permit application
One application under TCEQ permit numbers 184380, PSDTX1710, and GHGPSDTX275, filed Jun 12, 2026 on the expedited track. None granted. Rep. Darby's letter also lists HAP93, which does not appear in TCEQ's notice.389
Engines
240 gas engines with 180-foot stacks. Up to 200 would run at once, under a proposed yearly cap on engine-hours equal to 200 engines running full time.10
Power plant size
2,409 megawatts net, by the applicant's own figure for 200 engines running. That is enough for roughly 480,000 homes, by our estimate. None of it would go to the public.101112
Hazardous air pollutants
The applicant's own potential to emit: 697 tons a year, about 28 times the federal major-source threshold of 25 tons. Gas engines typically emit formaldehyde and acetaldehyde; the filings we could reach do not list the compounds.101314
Gas use
About 440 million cubic feet a day. The application does not state gas use or name a supplier; the Matterhorn Express pipeline crosses southern Tom Green County.1415Our estimate
Air modeling
The air modeling covers 200 engines running at once at full load, while 240 would be installed. The applicant proposes a yearly cap on engine-hours, not a limit on how many engines can run at the same time.10
Water
The developer told commissioners on Jun 2 the plan relies on an on-site well, clearing mesquite to free up water, and treating brackish water.16Developer statement, from news coverage
Tenant and investors
None disclosed.36
Public meetings
One community meeting, Apr 16, 2026, at The Four56 Church. Commissioners Court appearance Jun 2.161718
State pause
On Sep 21, 2026, the Governor directed TCEQ to issue no permits for data center projects until state grid and water audits are done.19
State representative
Rep. Drew Darby wrote TCEQ and Beacon on Aug 10 and asked TCEQ for a public meeting in Tom Green County. On Oct 8 he asked Beacon to withdraw its applications and said he will oppose each permit.91820

How loud would it be?

A screening estimate from our noise report, based on the engine maker's factory sound data, not a field measurement. It assumes all 240 engines running; with 200 running, levels would be about 1 dB lower. No noise study, noise data, or noise limit appears in the application, and TCEQ does not weigh noise in air permits.12

dBA is what a sound meter reports. It filters out the deep rumble, which carries furthest and passes through walls. The low-frequency reading (dBC) runs about 24 to 27 dB higher at every distance. Method and assumptions: our noise screening.1

What you can do

An air permit application is not an approval. Residents have a defined role in TCEQ's process.

  • Send a written comment to TCEQ on permit 184380 through its online comment system. A few sentences is enough. TCEQ must respond in writing to timely comments that are relevant to its decision, and commenting puts you on the mailing list for later notices. Any later hearing request must rest on issues raised in comments, so comment early and name your concerns.242526
  • If you live or own land nearby or downwind, you may qualify as an affected person who can request a contested case hearing, a trial-like proceeding before a state administrative law judge. Hearing requests are due 30 days after TCEQ mails its response to comments. Talk to an environmental attorney about deadlines.32526
  • Watch for the second newspaper notice. TCEQ issued the first notice in June 2026 (amended Jul 7), so comments can be sent now. If TCEQ drafts a permit, the Notice of Application and Preliminary Decision will be published and mailed, and comments close 30 days after it runs.323
  • Ask for permit conditions: a permanent public air monitor, a hard limit of 200 engines running at once, and testing for the hazardous pollutants.10
  • Watch the groundwater district for well applications on this tract.27
  • Read or request the file. The application can be viewed free at the Tom Green County Library's Angelo West Branch. TCEQ charges nothing to file a public information request; copies may carry a fee.328
  • Get an alert when a TCEQ notice is published: sign up.

This is general information, not legal advice.
