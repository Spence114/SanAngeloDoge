Debt that skips a vote

Certificates of obligation and tax notes need no election.Open
Not all debt goes to a vote. Bonds need an election. Certificates of obligation and tax notes do not: the City or County publishes a notice, and an election is required only if at least 5% of registered voters sign a petition.40 Since 2020 the City has issued certificates of obligation in 2020, 2022, 2023, and 2024, and tax notes in 2023, 2025, and 2026. We are matching each issue to the debt still owed, to show how much was voted on.41
