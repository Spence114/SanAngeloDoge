Governments/Development Corporation

City of San Angelo Development Corporation

A City-created corporation that spends a half-cent sales tax on economic development and voter-approved projects such as water supply. Its board approves grants to businesses and organizations.

A board appointed by City Council. Its agendas and financial statements are on the City's CivicClerk portal.Updated Nov 13, 2026

CityCountySAISDAppraisal DistrictDevelopment Corporation

Key numbers

Sales tax $12.8M

Its half-cent sales tax in FY2025, up from $11.2M in FY2022.1

Savings $29.0M

Unassigned fund balance in Jul 2026, up from $20.7M in May 2025.13

Chamber $476K

Paid each year to the San Angelo Chamber of Commerce.1

What the record shows
Monthly statements list the payments, such as $1 million to Angelo State University and $500,000 to Skyline Aviation. They do not list what each recipient agreed to do or whether it did.1
