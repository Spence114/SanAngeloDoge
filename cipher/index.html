Cipher data center

A 1-gigawatt data center proposed by Cipher Digital on private land just east of the Skybox site, running south along City Farm Road to the Red Creek substation. It is under county jurisdiction, so no city ordinance applies and the county has no zoning power.

At a glance

Much of what is known about this site comes from Cipher's Sep 22 briefing to the Commissioners Court. Those statements are shown in pink until they appear in a filing or agreement.

Developer
Cipher Digital Inc. (Nasdaq: CIFR), through Colchis LLC. Cipher bought 53% of Colchis LLC in late 2025 and raised its stake to 76% in early 2026 through capital contributions. The partner holding the remaining 24% is not named. Cipher's filings say clauses in the agreement could require it to buy out the partner's share after events Cipher does not control.234
Purchase date
Cipher's 2025 annual report says it bought into Colchis LLC in November 2025; the notes to its financial statements say October 2025.25
Site
Private land off US 67 North, running south down City Farm Road to the Red Creek substation, which sits in the middle of Cipher's land. "A few hundred acres" bought, with options toward about 600 acres. Price not disclosed.15
Size
1 GW requested from ERCOT.16
Power
AEP Texas grid at Red Creek. Cipher says it paid AEP a $17 million interconnection fee and holds a conditional utility approval.156
Grid status
In ERCOT's "Batch Zero" group; on hold until the state audit is done. Cipher expects word around Dec 7.167
Cooling and water
Closed loop with air-cooled chillers. About 2 million gallons of non-potable water (produced or brackish) trucked in to fill the loop, then about 5 gallons a day per employee. No drinking water for cooling, construction, or dust control. Cipher said it is not drilling drinking-water wells. Loop water treated and hauled off when changed.1Cipher at Commissioners Court, Sep 22, 2026
Backup power
Four-hour batteries, per Cipher. No TCEQ power plant application on file.18
Tenant
None signed for this site. Cipher's $5.5 billion, 15-year Amazon Web Services lease is for its Black Pearl site near Wink, in Winkler County, not this one.5910
Jobs
Company estimate: at least 100 permanent jobs, maybe 200 to 300, average pay $70,000 to $80,000. Construction would last almost two years; Commissioner Nanny asked about a workforce of roughly 1,000.1Cipher, Sep 22, 2026
Timeline
Company plan: design final in late 2026; construction from early 2027 if the audit clears; 14 to 20 months to build.1Cipher, Sep 22, 2026
Taxes
Cipher says it is not seeking an abatement, but suggested a tax agreement tied to operating rules.1Cipher, Sep 22, 2026
School district
Veribest ISD, as stated by Councilman Patrick Keely at the Sep 22 meeting. Not SAISD.1News coverage
County role
No county zoning. The county controls its roads, and Cipher needs a road-use agreement for City Farm Road.111
Company finances
First quarter 2026: net loss $114.3 million; total debt $4.73 billion; interest expense $59.2 million against $34.8 million of bitcoin mining revenue. Net losses in each of 2023, 2024, and 2025; accumulated deficit $1.118 billion.121314

What commissioners asked for on Sep 22

No vote was taken. Judge Lane Carter declined a private meeting, so the briefing was held in open court. From news coverage of the meeting.

Shawn Nanny
A full-time on-site ombudsman, a decommissioning bond, a Chapter 381 agreement with strict penalties up to shutdown, and at least 50% local construction hiring.1
Chad Decker
Groundwater pollution safeguards.1
Ralph Hoelscher
Opposes tax breaks and said he would be the first to vote against one.1
Lane Carter
Said any abatement would be handled like past wind and solar negotiations.1

How we got here

  1. Oct 2025Cipher buys 53% of Colchis LLC, announced Nov 3.29
  2. Early 2026Cipher's stake rises to 76% through capital contributions.3
  3. Mar 2026Land purchases. A residents' petition cites four tracts totaling more than 301 acres.15
  4. Aug 3, 2026State audit pauses ERCOT progress for data centers.7
  5. Aug 4, 2026Cipher tells investors it expects the project to be included in ERCOT's Batch Zero.6
  6. Aug 19, 2026Petition asks the Lipan-Kickapoo Water Conservation District to require Cipher's water plan before any well permit.15
  7. Sep 22, 2026Commissioners Court briefing. No vote.1
  8. DecemberERCOT decision expected after the audit, due Dec 10. Construction would follow in 2027 if cleared.116
