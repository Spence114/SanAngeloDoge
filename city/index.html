Governments/City

City of San Angelo

The City runs police, fire, water, sewer, streets, parks, and the airport. The City Council sets the budget and the tax rate, and hires the City Manager, who runs day-to-day operations.

City Council. Agendas, packets, minutes, and video are on the City's CivicClerk portal.Updated Nov 13, 2026

What to know

The figures and changes worth your attention.

-$6.1M

Last year ran short

The FY2026 budget, after revisions during the year, spent $6.1 million more than it took in.1

+71%

Health insurance

The City's contribution to the active employee health plan rises from $7.16 million to $12.27 million in one year. The health fund was $4.13 million in the red as of June 2026.1

$8.75/mo

New street fee

Per home, first billed Jan 1, 2027. Adopted 4 to 3. Street costs moved out of the General Fund into a new Right of Way Fund.23

60.7%

Firefighter pension

Funded at its Dec 31, 2023 valuation. The state has listed it as at risk of needing a funding restoration plan.45

$171.9M

Water and sewer plants

Capital plan estimates for the water treatment plant, water reclamation, and the Lake Nasworthy sewer.6

8 issues

Debt without a vote

Certificates of obligation and tax notes issued since 2020 without an election. Voters can force one only by petition.78

Open questions

What the record does not answer yet.

Investments Not yet asked

Where are the City's quarterly investment reports?

What the record shows
The City's investment policy, re-adopted each year, says investment reports are presented to Council at least quarterly and reviewed by an oversight committee. We found none on any Council agenda from 2017 to 2026.79
Who can answer
City Finance Department, City Manager
Audits Not yet asked

When did the Investment Oversight Committee and the Audit Committee last meet?

What the record shows
Members were appointed to both. No agendas or minutes for either are online, and the Audit Committee's portal page shows no meetings.7
Who can answer
City Clerk
Debt Not yet asked

How much of the debt still owed was approved by voters?

What the record shows
Since 2020 the City has issued certificates of obligation and tax notes, which need no election unless 5% of voters petition. The state's debt data does not say which debt was voted on.78
Who can answer
City Finance Department, County Auditor
Debt Not yet asked

What did the bonds behind the City's $53.8 million in refunding debt originally pay for?

What the record shows
The state lists $53.8 million of City debt as refunding: new bonds sold to pay off older ones. It does not say what the older bonds built.10
Who can answer
City Finance Department
Debt Not yet asked

Which streets and projects does the City's $67.0 million in transportation debt cover?

What the record shows
The state's category is transportation. The City's certificates of obligation in 2020 and 2022 were for streets. A project list is not in the state data.710
Who can answer
City Finance Department, Public Works
Privacy Not yet asked

Is statewide or national sharing turned on for San Angelo's Flock cameras, and how long are records kept?

What the record shows
Flock lets each agency choose whether other agencies can search its plate records, and how many days records are kept. The City's packet does not say which settings SAPD uses.1112
Who can answer
San Angelo Police Department

We send each question to the office named. Their answer is posted here as given. All open questions

What they post, and what is missing

A public body's records should be easy to find. Here is what we found.

  • PostedCouncil agendas, packets, and minutesPosted on CivicClerk, with video.
  • PartialAnnual audits (ACFR)Only FY2024 and FY2025 are on the City's site. Earlier years were accepted by Council but are not posted.
  • MissingQuarterly investment reports to CouncilRequired by the City's investment policy. None found on any Council agenda from 2017 to 2026.
  • MissingInvestment Oversight Committee recordsNo agendas or minutes found online.
  • MissingAudit Committee meetingsRe-chartered in Aug 2025. The portal category exists with no meetings posted.

From our review of the City's CivicClerk portal, Nov 2015 to Sept 2026, and the City's website.79
