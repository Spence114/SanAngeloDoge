San Angelo ISD

San Angelo's public school district. An elected Board of Trustees sets the budget and the tax rate and hires the superintendent. Most of its money comes from the state.

Board of Trustees. Agendas, minutes, and attachments since 2005 are on BoardBook.Updated Nov 13, 2026

Two debt figures

The 2025 school bonds

Proposition A, approved by voters on May 3, 2025.

Approved$397.0M

For new and rebuilt schools and safety work.7

Borrowed$385.1M

Sold in 2025, plus a premium of about $16.9 million.3

Interest on it$424.5M

More than the amount borrowed, paid through 2055.3

Unspent, Sep 30, 2026$361.5M

Still in the construction fund.4

Payments are back-loaded. Repayment of principal on the 2025 bonds does not begin until February 2028, and the largest payments come near the end, through 2055.3 Back-loading keeps early payments low and raises the total interest.

Open questions

What the record does not answer yet.

Debt Not yet asked

Why does SAISD list $473.3 million in obligations when its bonds total $446.8 million?

What the record shows
The district's transparency page gives $473.3 million in total obligations as of Aug 31, 2025. Its debt report lists $446.8 million in bonds.13
Who can answer
SAISD Financial Services
Investments Not yet asked

Where does the interest earned on unspent bond money go?

What the record shows
$361.5 million of bond money sat in the construction fund on Sep 30, 2026, earning about $1.2 million that month. Federal rules can require earnings above the bond rate to be paid back.4
Who can answer
SAISD Financial Services
Schools Not yet asked

What enrollment forecast were the new school buildings sized for?

What the record shows
Enrollment fell from 14,100 in 2021 to 13,004 in 2025, as the district reports it, while voters approved $397 million for new and rebuilt schools in May 2025.17
Who can answer
SAISD Superintendent, Board of Trustees
Debt Not yet asked

How much of the $397 million voters approved can still be borrowed?

What the record shows
SAISD sold $385.1 million of the $397 million authorized, plus a premium of about $16.9 million. Whether the premium counts against the authorization decides what is left.37
Who can answer
SAISD Financial Services
